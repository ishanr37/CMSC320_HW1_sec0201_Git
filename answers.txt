Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git is useful for incremental code development and teamwork with other developers.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science could be applied in quantitative finance, specifically for the purpose of algorithmic trading.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging creates a dedicated merge commit and preserves the individual branch histories, while rebase copies the unique commits from one branch onto the latest state of another branch.
